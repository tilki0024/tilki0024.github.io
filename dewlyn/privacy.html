Privacy Policy

Dewlyn · Last updated 2 November 2026

Dewlyn does not collect your data. It is also free, with nothing to buy inside it, so there is no payment information either.

There is no account to create, no server to send anything to, and no analytics. Everything the app makes stays on your device. The rest of this page explains what that means in detail, because a policy that only says "we care about privacy" tells you nothing.

Face data

This section exists because the App Store asks every app that photographs a face to say exactly what it does with that data. Here it is in full.

What face data the app collects. Dewlyn takes two photographs of your face per session — one before a routine and one after — and only when you tap the shutter yourself. It does not create or store any biometric template, faceprint, face embedding, facial landmark, measurement or geometric map of your face. It performs no face recognition, no face detection, no identification, no age or emotion estimation, and no analysis or scoring of your appearance. The photographs are ordinary image files and nothing is derived from them.

How it is used. The two photographs are displayed to you, side by side, so you can see the difference yourself. That is their only purpose. They are not used for training, analytics, advertising, personalisation, research or any other purpose.

Where it is stored. Only inside Dewlyn's own folder in your device's app storage. The photographs are not written to your camera roll or photo library, are not readable by other apps, and are never transmitted off the device. Dewlyn has no server, no account system, and no network code; on Android the app does not even request the internet permission, so it is incapable of uploading anything.

Whether it is shared. No. Face data is never shared with us, with third parties, with analytics providers, with advertisers, or with anyone else. There is no third-party SDK in the app that receives it. The only way any image leaves your device is if you yourself tap Share and choose an app to send it to, which is your action and your choice.

How long it is retained. For as long as you keep it, and no longer. The photographs stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Nothing expires them and nothing copies them elsewhere.

How it is deleted. Press and hold any session in the app and confirm, and both photographs for that session are permanently erased from the device. Deleting the app removes everything at once. Because nothing was ever collected by us, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Your photos

The before and after photos are taken by the app and written to Dewlyn's own folder inside your device's app storage. They are:

  • never uploaded anywhere;
  • not written to your camera roll or photo library;
  • not readable by other apps;
  • deleted together with the app if you uninstall it.

The camera is used only while a capture screen is open, and only to take the photo you tap for. There is no face recognition, no analysis of your appearance, and no scoring of how you look.

When you share

If you tap Share, Dewlyn builds a single image from your two photos and hands it to the iOS share sheet. What happens next is up to you and to the app you choose to send it to. Dewlyn does not post anything on your behalf and does not keep a copy anywhere other than on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • your photos and the list of sessions;
  • your streak, and the date of your last session;
  • whether you have Pro, and your reminder time;
  • whether you have finished the introduction.

That is the complete list.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled by your device itself. There are no push notifications, no notification server, and no device token — so nothing about your reminder leaves your phone.

Children

Dewlyn is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them — it does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. You can remove everything the app holds by deleting Dewlyn from your device.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships and the change will be described here in plain language, not buried.
